Research Experience

Cintra, a Ferrovial Company · 2026 – Present
Travel Behavior Impacts of Autonomous Personal Vehicle Technology
Designing, administering, and analyzing a survey targeting ~1,500 workers in three metropolitan areas to assess willingness to pay to save travel time. Also collecting qualitative, subjective insights into how AV technology may influence travel behavior.
Cintra, a Ferrovial Company · 2025 – Present
Driverless Taxis: Adoption, Intentions, Perceptions, and Travel Behavior Impacts
Designed a large-scale survey (n ≈ 2,200) across four major metropolitan areas (Phoenix, San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Austin) to examine robotaxi adoption — including sociodemographic factors, usage contexts, and barriers/motivators. Findings featured in Ferrovial's newsroom.
Cintra, a Ferrovial Company · 2022 – 2024
Pandemic-Related Telework and Travel Behavior Impacts
Contributed to design, administration, data cleaning, and analysis of three large-scale studies: the Work from Home (WFH) Survey (Waves 2 & 3) and the Nonwork Travel Survey (NWTS). Explored teleworking changes, commuting patterns, and nonwork travel among a representative sample.
Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, South Korea · 2021 – 2022
Self-Driving Public Transportation Traffic Support Technology Tailored to Individual Users
Planned and executed a campus-based and national stated-preference survey to analyze users' behavior and satisfaction regarding self-driving public transportation services.
Korea Development Institute · 2020 – 2022
Gimhae Airport–Daedong Expansion: Transportation Demand Estimation & Economic Analysis
Estimated future demands and benefits of a highway expansion project. Built a database and developed command macros on EMME/4 for modal split and assignments. Automated financial feasibility reporting using Python.
Metropolitan Transport Commission, South Korea · 2020 – 2021
BRT Express Bus System Basic Plan Revision
Reconstructed the national BRT express bus system basic plan: re-established vision and role, adjusted existing routes, discovered new routes, and evaluated priority using network analysis, demand estimation (web-crawling via MATLAB), potential demand analysis, and facility standards.
Yonsei University Future-Leading Research Initiative · 2020 – 2022
Smart Mobility User Behavior Analysis and Predictive Model Development
Conducted multiple studies on smart mobility user behavior and developed materials for the "First/Last Mile Network Establishment Research Forum" by the Gyeonggi Construction Corporation.
Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, South Korea · 2020
Policy Feasibility Analysis: Highway Construction Projects (3 Cases)
Analyzed the impact of proposed highway lines (Seoul-Yangpyeong, Jecheon-Yeongwol, Ganghwa-Gyeyang) on existing road networks, examining employment effects, economic impacts, environmental considerations, and safety evaluations.
