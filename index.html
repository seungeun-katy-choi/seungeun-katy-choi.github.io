About Me

I’m deeply interested in the many dimensions of travel behavior—the complex ways in which individuals’ decisions to travel (or not) are shaped by technological, environmental, and social influences. After completing my B.A. in Urban Planning and Engineering at Yonsei University, I pursued a Master’s degree and then ventured to Georgia Tech to further explore telework, driverless taxis, and next-generation transportation systems.

My background in Civil and Environmental Engineering and Computational Science & Engineering allows me to combine traditional transportation theories with advanced data analytics. I’m especially fascinated by how emerging technologies—like remote work, shared mobility, and autonomous vehicles—are reshaping travel demand, influencing time use, and ultimately transforming urban life. If you’re interested in travel behavior modeling or the future of urban mobility, I’d love to connect!
