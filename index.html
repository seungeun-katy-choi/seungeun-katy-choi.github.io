Research

Ongoing · Georgia Tech
Telework & Travel Behavior in the Post-Pandemic Era
Investigating how remote and hybrid work arrangements reshape travel demand, time use, and urban mobility patterns. Using multi-year survey data from two U.S. regions to track attitudinal and behavioral shifts from pre- to late-pandemic periods.
Ongoing · Georgia Tech
Non-Commute Activity & Travel Demand Modeling
Examining how individuals' non-commute activity engagement—shopping, leisure, social—has evolved through the pandemic and what factors (attitudinal, sociodemographic, technological) drive these changes.
Completed · Yonsei University · 2021–2022
Free-Floating E-Bike Sharing & COVID-19
Applied unsupervised machine learning to GPS-level trip data to characterize shifts in shared electric bicycle use across Seoul and Sejong before and during the COVID-19 pandemic. Published in Cities (2023).
Completed · Yonsei University · 2020–2021
On-Demand Mobility & Commute Mode Choice
Hybrid choice modeling of commuter preferences for on-demand mobility services in South Korea, accounting for working flexibility and attitudinal factors. Presented at TRB 2022.
